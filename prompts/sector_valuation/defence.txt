EBITDA = Revenue × target EBITDA margin (defence margins are sticky; minimal RM volatility)
PAT = (EBITDA - D&A - Interest) × 0.75
EPS = PAT / Shares Outstanding

Cross-check: EV/EBITDA 25-40x for premium defence

Warning: Defence P/E can look expensive on trailing but orders justify forward premium.
Always use FORWARD earnings for defence — lumpiness distorts trailing metrics.
