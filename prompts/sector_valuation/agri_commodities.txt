{company_data}

STEP 1 — EXTRACT SECTOR KPIs
- Primary commodity traded/processed (corn, cashew, spices, etc.)
- Volume (MT/year) — growth
- Procurement price (₹/MT or ₹/quintal) and selling price — spread
- Processing/value-add margin (₹/MT) — processing > trading margin
- Storage capacity (MT) and fill rate — competitive moat in fragmented market
- Export revenue % and key markets
  — Export = USD-denominated = INR depreciation tailwind
- Government export/import policy sensitivity (the biggest risk for agri commodities)
- Value-added product % (snacks, flour, flakes vs raw commodity)
- EBITDA margin — trading: 3-6%; processing: 8-14%; branded: 15-22%
- Working capital: inventory days (high for commodity cos — watch financing cost)
- Crop season dependency (monsoon risk — H1/H2 seasonality)

STEP 2 — FORWARD REVENUE & EARNINGS ESTIMATE (FY27E)
Revenue = Volume × blended realisation per MT
EBITDA = Revenue × target EBITDA margin
        (OR Volume × EBITDA per MT if spread-driven business)
PAT = (EBITDA - D&A - Interest) × 0.75
EPS = PAT / Shares Outstanding
Note: Commodity businesses are lumpy — use 3-year average EBITDA margin, not peak

STEP 3 — VALUATION
Primary method: Forward P/E on normalised FY27E EPS
  - Branded/value-added agri products (predictable): 20-30x P/E
  - Quality processor (consistent spread, storage moat): 12-18x P/E
  - Commodity trader (thin spread, policy risk): 7-12x P/E
Cross-check: EV/EBITDA 6-14x (use normalised EBITDA, not peak-cycle)

Base: Volume grows 10-15%; spread stable; policy environment normal
Bear: Government bans exports; bumper crop globally; spread compressed; WC stress

Buy more when: Export policy eases; commodity prices rising with procurement locked in; value-add ramp
