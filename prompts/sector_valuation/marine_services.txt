STEP 1 — EXTRACT SECTOR KPIs
- Fleet profile:

  - Fleet utilisation % — target >80%; <65% signals idle capacity drag
- Order book (₹Cr) and order book-to-trailing revenue ratio

  Goa Shipyard, RVNL, state PWDs, Navy
  - Client concentration risk: if IWAI >70% of revenue, watch policy/budget risk
- Revenue per vessel (₹Cr) — efficiency metric
- EBITDA margin:
  - Asset-light (survey, O&M services): 28-38%
  - Asset-heavy (capital dredging, fleet hire): 20-30%
  - Watch for cost overruns on fixed-price government contracts
- Working capital days: government contracts = slow payment (90-180 days typical)
  - High receivables = cash flow strain; watch debtors as % of revenue
- Net debt / EBITDA — fleet expansion is capex-intensive; >3x is concerning
- New fleet addition / capex plan and expected commissioning timeline
- Jal Marg Vikas Project (JMVP), PM GatiShakti, Sagarmala programme — policy tailwinds
- Revenue from hydrographic surveys, engineering consultancy (asset-light, high margin)

STEP 2 — FORWARD REVENUE & EARNINGS ESTIMATE (FY27E)
Revenue = (Fleet size × Utilisation % × Day rate) + O&M contract recurring revenue
         + Survey / consultancy fee income
EBITDA = Revenue × target EBITDA margin
PAT = (EBITDA - D&A - Interest) × 0.75
EPS = PAT / Shares Outstanding

STEP 3 — VALUATION
Primary method: Forward P/E on FY27E (preferred for high-margin government services companies)
  - Asset-light marine services (survey, O&M, high government contract visibility): 25-35x P/E
  - Mixed (dredging + services, moderate visibility): 18-28x P/E
  - Asset-heavy capital dredger (tender-to-tender, fleet replacement risk): 12-18x P/E
Cross-check: Forward EV/EBITDA — 10-18x (lower end for pure dredging)
Note: Small-cap marine services companies with IWAI/govt monopoly contracts command
      scarcity premium; P/E rerating is driven by order book announcements.

Bull: New JMVP / Sagarmala project wins; fleet expansion commissioned; IWAI budget hike; survey scope expands
Base: Steady IWAI contract renewal; utilisation 80%; O&M revenue grows 15% CAGR; margin stable
Bear: Delayed government payments stretch working capital; contract not renewed; dry dock or vessel breakdown; capex overrun
