STEP 1 — EXTRACT SECTOR KPIs

- Realisation per MT (₹/MT) — by grade if disclosed; trend over last 4 quarters
- Wastepaper procurement cost (₹/MT) — largest input cost (50-60% of revenue)

- Energy cost per MT — paper mills are coal/gas/steam intensive; 15-25% of revenue
- Conversion spread = Realisation per MT - Wastepaper cost per MT - Energy cost per MT

- EBITDA margin: good range 12-18% for packaging grades, 8-12% for newsprint/W&P

- Net debt / EBITDA: paper mills are capex-heavy; watch >3x as a red flag
- Capital expansion plans: new machines, de-inking plant, tissue capacity

STEP 2 — DEMAND DRIVERS & REVENUE ESTIMATE (FY27E)
Revenue = Capacity (MT) × Utilisation % × Realisation per MT
Key demand drivers:
  - E-commerce boom → corrugated packaging → kraft/testliner/fluting demand
  - FMCG & pharma → duplex board (carton packaging) demand
  - Education & government printing → W&P demand
  - Cigarette industry → ivory board demand
  - Regulatory tailwinds: plastic ban → paper packaging substitution
EBITDA = Revenue × target EBITDA margin (or Volume × target conversion spread per MT)
PAT = (EBITDA - D&A - Interest) × 0.75
EPS = PAT / Shares Outstanding

STEP 3 — VALUATION
Primary method: Forward EV/EBITDA on FY27E

Cross-check: Forward P/E 10-18x (use lower end for commodity grades)
