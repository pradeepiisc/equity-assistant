## Margin Commentary
Management was explicit that margin volatility is mix-driven, with machines being thin-margin but strategic for annuity creation. Core consumables and manufacturing businesses were stated to deliver stable 13–17% EBITDA margins, while consolidated EBITDA is guided at ~15% on average and PAT at ~10%. The explanation was internally consistent and supported by disclosed mix shifts in H1.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Revenue growth is positioned as largely organic, driven by increased adoption of digital textile printing, higher machine installations, and scaling of paper and ink volumes. Management consistently emphasized the flywheel effect where machines drive future consumables. Growth drivers cited include viscose fabric adoption, government subsidies (~35%), and long-term shift from conventional to digital printing.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- Seasonality in textile demand, with Q1 typically weakest (H1FY26)
- Margin volatility due to higher proportion of low-margin machine sales (H1FY26)
- Need for higher working capital as revenues scale and credit cycles extend (H1FY26)
