# Transcript Analysis — Saakshi Medtech & Panels Ltd (SAAKSHI)

## Executive Summary
Across the three calls (Nov 2023, Dec 2024, Nov 2025), Saakshi Medtech shows a clear U-shaped trajectory: optimistic growth and diversification plans in FY24, a sharp operational and revenue setback in H1 FY25 due to customer-side deferrals, followed by a strong recovery and margin expansion in H1 FY26. Management credibility improves in the latest call, with earlier deferred themes (capacity consolidation, aerospace ramp-up) translating into measurable delivery. Key themes include high customer concentration risk, gradual diversification into aerospace, EV and data-center linked fabrication, and a decisive shift toward higher-margin, value-added segments. Execution risk remains, but FY26 commentary shows better specificity and confidence backed by numbers.

## Guidance vs Delivery
### Q2FY24 — MISSED
**Promised:** 15–20% revenue growth in FY24 and ~20% CAGR for next couple of years; EBITDA margins to be maintained.
**Delivered:** FY24 ended flat at ~₹121 Cr; H1FY25 revenue declined 27% YoY.

### H1FY25 — DELIVERED
**Promised:** Deferred orders to resume from Apr–May 2025; recovery from H1FY26.
**Delivered:** H1FY26 revenue up 31% YoY; EBITDA margin expanded to 18.7%.

## Margin Commentary
Margins were initially guided as ‘maintainable’ in FY24. H1FY25 saw margin pressure due to operating deleverage and revenue decline, though cost control was emphasized. In H1FY26, EBITDA expanded sharply to ~18.7%, driven by operating leverage, higher contribution from aerospace and engineered assemblies, and facility consolidation. Margin guidance now sounds more credible, with management explicitly stating sustainability rather than aggressive expansion.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
The growth story evolved from steady OEM-led expansion (renewables, panels) to a disruption-led pause in FY25 due to customer deferrals (GE Vernova, textile OEM). Subsequently, growth drivers shifted toward aerospace, EV panels (Mahindra), and data-center linked fabrication (radiators). The narrative has shifted from broad optimism to segment-specific, execution-backed growth.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- High dependence on a few large OEM customers; order deferrals directly impact revenue (Q2FY24, H1FY25).
- Regulatory changes in medical devices slowing scale-up (Q2FY24, H1FY25).
- Long qualification and certification cycles in aerospace delaying revenue realization (H1FY25, H1FY26).
- Project-based businesses (data centers, defense) have long tendering and RFQ cycles (H1FY25).

## Positive Signals
- First Saakshi-branded X-ray system received AERB, BIS, CDSCO approvals; revenue expected from Q4FY24 (Q2FY24).
- Export share increased from ~2.5% FY23 to over 5% by FY25 (H1FY25).
- Aerospace revenue grew ~7x YoY in H1FY26 (H1FY26).
- EBITDA margin expansion to ~18.7% in H1FY26 (H1FY26).
- ₹250 Cr, 5-year EV panel order from Mahindra with ~₹230 Cr yet to be executed (H1FY26).

## Red Flags
- Repeated deferral of large customer orders (GE Vernova, textile OEM) across multiple quarters without firm restart dates (H1FY25).
- Very high customer concentration acknowledged (top 5 = ~90–95% revenue) with slow diversification (Q2FY24).
- Limited long-term order book visibility (only 3-month visibility cited repeatedly) – ‘we don’t have a one-year order book’ (H1FY26).
