# Transcript Analysis — Rashi Peripherals Ltd (RPTECH)

## Executive Summary
Across FY26, Rashi Peripherals delivered a strong recovery-led growth cycle, with core run‑rate business consistently growing 15–30% excluding large one‑off project deals, supported by PC refresh, AI-led premiumization and price tailwinds. Management demonstrated improving execution discipline—prioritizing ROCE over headline revenue—while selectively stepping back from low-margin large projects in FY26. Margins expanded meaningfully in H2FY26 driven by mix, operating leverage and pricing, with FY26 EBITDA margin reaching ~2.9%. Overall, transcripts reflect improving management confidence, better working capital control, and a clearer capital allocation framework, though reliance on cyclical pricing tailwinds remains a key risk.

## Guidance Evolution Across Quarters
*Management guidance shows realism and discipline—targets are conservative, explained repeatedly, and not quietly diluted.*

### EBITDA margin target — Gradually Revised
- **Q1FY26:** 2.5–2.7% is steady-state; 3.5% is not sustainable long term.
- **Q2FY26:** Annualized margin of ~2.7% remains the right expectation.
- **Q3FY26:** Margins benefiting from pricing and operating leverage.
- **Q4FY26:** FY26 EBITDA margin reached ~2.9% due to mix and leverage.

*Assessment: Management cautiously raised realized margins while maintaining conservative long-term language.*

### Large project / data center deals — Consistent
- **Q1FY26:** Judicious participation due to margin and ROCE concerns.
- **Q2FY26:** Waiting for full collection before taking new large projects.
- **Q3FY26:** No large deals in FY26 by design; pipeline exists.
- **Q4FY26:** FY27 participation planned subject to ROI and capital discipline.

*Assessment: Clear and disciplined capital allocation approach with no backtracking.*

## Margin Commentary
Across quarters, management consistently emphasized that distribution margins are structurally thin and percentage-based. Margin expansion in FY26 was attributed to favorable product mix (run-rate vs projects), pricing-led operating leverage, and cost discipline rather than structural margin reset. Credibility is high as management repeatedly cautioned against extrapolating peak quarterly margins and anchored expectations around ~2.7–3% EBITDA.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Revenue growth narrative evolved from run-rate replacement of prior-year large projects (Q1–Q2) to price- and AI-led acceleration in H2. Management consistently distinguished organic run-rate growth from project-led spikes, highlighting PC refresh, AI PCs, component price hikes, enterprise demand and Tier‑2/3 penetration. Narrative remained coherent and internally consistent.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- Component shortages and price volatility impacting affordability (Q3FY26)
- Extended refresh cycles if prices rise too sharply (Q3FY26)
- Lower ROCE and intense competition in large data center deals (Q1FY26, Q4FY26)
- Potential consumer demand softness due to affordability in H2FY27 (Q4FY26)

## Positive Signals
- EBITDA margin expansion to ~3% in FY26 (Q4FY26)
- Operating cash flow of INR 514 Cr generated in FY26 (Q4FY26)
- ROCE crossed 16% annually and ~19% in Q4 (Q4FY26)
- Dell commercial partnership scaling toward double-digit revenue share (Q3FY26, Q4FY26)
- Embedded/semiconductor business grew 131% Y-o-Y (Q4FY26)

## Red Flags
- Heavy reliance on price hikes rather than volume growth in H2 (Q3FY26 commentary)
- Management acknowledged potential consumer affordability risk if laptop prices rise to INR 70k+ (Q4FY26)
- Semiconductor business margins attractive but working capital intensive (Q4FY26)

## Overall Investment Signal
**positive: Strong execution, improving ROCE, disciplined capital allocation and structural demand tailwinds outweigh cyclical pricing risks, though sustainability beyond the price-led supercycle must be monitored.**
