# Transcript Analysis — M&B Engineering Ltd (MBEL)

## Executive Summary
Across Q1–Q3 FY26, M&B Engineering delivered strong revenue growth, driven by export scaling (US, Canada) and a robust domestic order book, while navigating tariff-led margin volatility. Management consistently emphasized disciplined growth, capacity-led execution, and margin protection over aggressive topline chasing. Guidance on FY26 revenue (~₹1,225–1,250 cr) and EBITDA margin (~12.75–13%) has remained largely stable, with delivery tracking expectations so far. Key themes include export-led margin uplift, capacity constraints at Sanand, gradual Cheyyar ramp-up, and cautious optimism amid tariff and FX headwinds.

## Margin Commentary
Margins have been a central discussion point, with management consistently attributing pressure to US sectoral tariffs (50%), FX volatility on Proflex imports, and export freight costs. While export EBITDA remains structurally higher than domestic, management admitted to absorbing some tariffs to build US relationships. Credibility is supported by clear quantification of tariff impact (e.g., ₹2.85 cr in Q2FY26) and realistic full-year EBITDA guidance of ~12.75–13%.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Revenue growth is largely organic, driven by strong order inflows in Phenix (PEB) and Proflex roofing, with exports emerging as a key incremental driver. Management consistently cited industrial capex, railways, agri-warehousing, and North American demand as growth drivers. The narrative has remained consistent, with capacity ramp-up (Cheyyar) and brownfield expansions underpinning medium-term growth.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- US sectoral tariffs impacting export margins (Q1FY26, Q2FY26, Q3FY26)
- FX volatility on imported Proflex raw materials (Q2FY26)
- Capacity constraints at Sanand until expansion comes online (Q2FY26, Q3FY26)
- Execution delays due to monsoon and site readiness (Q2FY26, Q3FY26)

## Positive Signals
- Order book grew to ₹1,059 cr by Dec 2025, up 38% YoY (Q3FY26)
- Single largest export order of ₹212 cr won in the US (Q3FY26)
- Exports up 107% YoY to ₹120 cr in 9MFY26 (Q3FY26)
- Debt largely repaid post-IPO; near debt-free balance sheet (Q1FY26)

## Red Flags
- Repeated emphasis on tariff uncertainty limiting margin visibility beyond FY26
- Working capital days increased sharply post-IPO due to inventory and creditor changes (Q1FY26, Q2FY26)
- Dependence on Sanand plant certifications creates temporary export bottleneck

## Overall Investment Signal
**cautious: Strong growth visibility and execution track record are offset by near-term margin headwinds from tariffs and FX, warranting a balanced stance.**
