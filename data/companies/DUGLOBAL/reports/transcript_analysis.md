# Transcript Analysis — DU Digital Global Ltd (DUGLOBAL)

## Executive Summary
Across FY25 and H1FY26, DU Digital Global has delivered strong top-line growth with sharp margin expansion, supported by execution of new MEA contracts in South Korea and Thailand and operating leverage from scaling platforms. Management communication remains consistent and confident, repeatedly emphasizing technology-led outsourcing, DuVerify deployment, and global expansion. Margins, which dipped in FY25 due to upfront hiring and setup costs, rebounded sharply in H1FY26, validating earlier investment rationale. Overall, the transcripts signal improving execution quality, disciplined scaling, and increasing credibility of the growth narrative.

## Management Tone — IMPROVING
- FY25 Investor Presentation (Jun 2025): Management acknowledged EBITDA margin moderation due to "higher employee costs, addition of KMPs, and strategic investments to drive future growth," signalling upfront candour.
- FY25 Investor Presentation (Jun 2025): Chairman stated, "Our journey from a legacy travel business to a global mobility tech company has only just begun," outlining a clear transformation narrative.
- H1FY26 Investor Presentation (Nov 2025): Management highlighted tangible outcomes, noting "Consolidated EBITDA stood at Rs 82 Mn in H1FY26 (Margin of 27%)" as a result of new verticals and organic growth.
- H1FY26 Investor Presentation (Nov 2025): Forward-looking but measured tone: "DuVerify successfully implemented in Georgia and South Korea, proving global scalability."

## Guidance vs Delivery
### FY25 (guidance given in Jun 2025) — EXCEEDED
**Promised:** Expansion in South Korea and Thailand, scaling DuVerify, and short-term margin pressure due to investments.
**Delivered:** By H1FY26, ICACs in South Korea and Thailand were operational and EBITDA margin expanded to 26.7% consolidated.

## Guidance Evolution Across Quarters
*Across both periods, management set achievable milestones and reported delivery with specificity. There is little evidence of guidance dilution or deferral.*

### Margin recovery after investment phase — Consistent
- **FY25:** EBITDA moderated to 13% due to higher employee costs and strategic investments.
- **H1FY26:** EBITDA margin expanded to 26.7% consolidated due to new verticals and operating leverage.

*Assessment: Management clearly signposted near-term margin pressure followed by recovery, which materialised faster than expected.*

### International expansion via MEA contracts — Consistent
- **FY25:** Secured 3-year MEA contracts in Seoul and Thailand; centres being launched.
- **H1FY26:** ICACs in South Korea and Thailand successfully opened and operating.

*Assessment: Execution tracked guidance with no visible slippage.*

## Margin Commentary
In FY25, management explicitly attributed EBITDA margin compression to deliberate hiring, leadership additions, and upfront costs of new VACs and ICACs. By H1FY26, margins rebounded sharply to 26–27% at the consolidated level, which management credited to operating leverage and contribution from new verticals. The explanation across quarters is internally consistent, enhancing credibility of margin commentary.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Revenue growth has been positioned as largely organic, driven by new government contracts (South Korea, Thailand), expansion of VAC footprint, and scaling of technology platforms like DuVerify. The narrative consistently emphasizes structural tailwinds from outsourcing and digitalisation of visa services rather than one-off wins, with no major shifts in driver mix across the two periods.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- Dependence on government tenders and contract renewals, highlighted through repeated references to MEA and embassy contracts (FY25, H1FY26).
- Upfront cost pressure from manpower expansion and new centre launches impacting margins in the short term (FY25).
- Execution risk in technology migration projects such as CRM and Microsoft Dynamics 365 implementation (FY25, H1FY26).

## Positive Signals
- 65% YoY consolidated revenue growth in FY25 (Jun 2025).
- EBITDA margin expansion to 26.7% in H1FY26 from 13.3% in FY25 (Nov 2025).
- Operational launch of ICACs in South Korea and Thailand (H1FY26).
- Successful deployment of DuVerify in Georgia and South Korea proving scalability (H1FY26).

## Red Flags
- Heavy reliance on employee-led expansion, with employee benefits expense rising sharply in FY25 (173% YoY standalone), indicating execution intensity.
- Limited quantitative forward revenue guidance despite strong growth narrative; projections remain largely qualitative.

## Overall Investment Signal
**positive: Strong revenue momentum, sharp margin recovery, and consistent execution against stated priorities suggest improving quality of earnings and management credibility.**
