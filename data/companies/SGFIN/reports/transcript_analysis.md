# Transcript Analysis — SG Finserve Ltd (SGFIN)

## Executive Summary
Across the six quarters, SG Finserve has delivered rapid AUM growth driven by anchor-led supply chain finance, but the narrative has become more cautious and less consistent over time. Early calls (FY25) showed high confidence, aggressive growth targets (₹4,000–6,000 crore AUM) and strong conviction around ROE/ROA, backed by zero NPAs and anchor expansion. From Q2–Q3 FY26 onward, management tone shifted due to macro slowdown, leadership churn, and repeated guidance downgrades, especially on PAT. While asset quality discipline and capital adequacy remain strong positives, evolving guidance, management exits, and investor confusion around long-term plans have weakened credibility signals despite solid operating performance.

## Management Tone — DETERIORATING
- Q3 FY25: Management stated they were "more confident than last earnings call" about achieving ₹5,000–6,000 crore loan book (Jan 2025).
- Q1 FY26: Confident guidance of ₹3,500–4,000 crore AUM by FY26 and ₹6,000 crore by FY27 with 15–18% ROE (Jul 2025).
- Q2 FY26: Admission that PAT guidance of ₹150–160 crore is now revised to ₹120–125 crore due to macro slowdown (Oct 2025).
- Q3 FY26: Investors explicitly called guidance "confusing" and management acknowledged repeated revisions and agreed communication needs improvement (Jan 2026).
- Q3 FY26: Management clarified that long-term guidance is intentionally conservative due to leadership transition and past regulatory disruption (Jan 2026).

### Q4FY25 — PENDING

### Q1FY26 — MISSED

## Guidance Evolution Across Quarters
*Management initially set aggressive, aspirational targets and later revised them downward citing macro slowdown, regulatory disruption, and leadership transition. While explanations are provided, repeated revisions signal optimism bias in early guidance.*

### Loan book scale-up to ₹6,000 crore — Gradually Revised
- **Q2FY25:** Target of ₹6,000 crore loan book by FY27 reiterated with confidence.
- **Q4FY25:** Visibility to scale from ₹2,300 crore to ₹6,000 crore by FY27.
- **Q1FY26:** Reaffirmed ₹6,000 crore by FY27 with strong anchor pipeline.
- **Q3FY26:** Guidance reframed to 20% CAGR, ₹7,500 crore by FY30, ₹3,500 crore by FY26.

*Assessment: Targets remain directionally intact but timelines have been pushed out and reframed more conservatively.*

### PAT / profitability guidance — Reversed
- **Q4FY25:** Run-rate PAT of ₹100 crore+ achievable.
- **Q1FY26:** FY26 PAT guided at ₹150–160 crore.
- **Q2FY26:** FY26 PAT revised down to ₹120–125 crore.

*Assessment: Clear downward revision within two quarters, reducing confidence in near-term earnings visibility.*

## Margin Commentary
Margins have been discussed with relative consistency: yield around 12–13%, cost of borrowing declining from ~8.5% toward ~8%, and spreads of ~4%. Management repeatedly emphasized lagged pass-through of rate cuts as supportive to NIMs (Q4FY25, Q1FY26). However, Q2 FY26 saw yield compression to ~11.5% due to onboarding large anchors like Tata Motors and M&M at lower pricing, which management framed as strategic and temporary. Overall margin commentary is detailed and credible, though actual ROE remains below long-term targets due to low leverage.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Revenue growth is positioned as largely organic, driven by anchor onboarding, deeper penetration of existing anchors, and faster churn cycles (30–35 days). The narrative has been consistent around supply chain finance dominance (70–85% of AUM), with limited diversification into non-SCF products. Over time, management acknowledged macro headwinds in construction, auto, and consumer sectors, which tempered growth expectations in FY26, marking a shift from earlier bullish growth assumptions.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- Macro slowdown impacting anchor volumes and dealer demand (Q4FY25, Q2FY26).
- Regulatory risk highlighted through earlier Type II license delay causing book contraction (Q2FY25, Q3FY26).
- Management transition risk acknowledged explicitly during Q2–Q3 FY26 (Oct 2025, Jan 2026).
- Potential NPAs acknowledged as possible though historically nil (Q3FY25, Q2FY26).

## Positive Signals
- Loan book doubled QoQ in Q3 FY25 post RBI Type II license (Q3FY25).
- Zero GNPA maintained across all quarters despite ₹50,000+ crore cumulative disbursements (multiple quarters).
- Anchor base expanded to 45+ corporates including Tata, Adani, Vedanta, JSW (Q4FY25, Q1FY26).
- Factoring license granted by RBI, expanding SCF product suite (Q3FY26).
- Cost-to-income maintained below 15% with lean employee base (Q3FY26).

## Red Flags
- Repeated PAT guidance revisions within short timeframes ("₹150 crore PAT" later revised to ₹120–125 crore).
- Investor feedback explicitly calling guidance and diversification plans "confusing" (Q3FY26).
- Simultaneous exit of CEO and CFO/COO raised continuity concerns (Q2FY26).
- Long-term guidance (FY30) given despite admission of near-term uncertainty (Q3FY26).

## Overall Investment Signal
**cautious: Strong underlying business model and asset quality discipline, but repeated guidance revisions, leadership churn, and communication gaps reduce near-term confidence despite long-term opportunity.**
