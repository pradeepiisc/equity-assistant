# Transcript Analysis — Quadrant Future Tek Ltd (QUADFUTURE)

## Executive Summary
Q1FY26 was a transition quarter for Quadrant Future Tek, marked by modest cable-led revenue growth (+12% YoY) and continued losses in the Kavach signalling division due to certification-related delays. Management communication was confident and detailed on execution readiness, capacity, and approvals, but cautious to evasive on margins and financial outcomes for Kavach. The strategic pivot is clearly towards large-scale railway safety systems, with cables positioned as a cash-generating stabiliser. Overall, the opportunity size is large, but near-term financial visibility hinges almost entirely on timely ISA certification.

## Margin Commentary
Margins were discussed only for the cable business, where EBITDA improved to 8.9% from 7.3% YoY. Management repeatedly declined to comment on Kavach margins, citing authorisation constraints, which limits financial modelling credibility despite operational confidence.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Current revenues are entirely cable-driven, with growth described as seasonal and back-ended. The future growth narrative is overwhelmingly Kavach-led, supported by a ₹998 Cr order book, while cables are positioned as a stable base expanding into solar, EV, and exports.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- Delay in ISA certification impacting Kavach rollout (Q1FY26).
- High upfront costs and losses in Kavach division before revenue commencement (Q1FY26).
- Dependence on railway regulatory approvals and testing timelines (Q1FY26).

## Positive Signals
- ₹998 Cr Kavach order book secured including loco and trackside orders (Q1FY26).
- Production capacity of ~300 locomotives per month already built (Q1FY26).
- Appointment of experienced CEO for cable division to revive growth (Q1FY26).
- Improvement in EBITDA margin in cable business to 8.9% (Q1FY26).

## Red Flags
- Repeated refusal to discuss Kavach margins: 'I am not authorised to comment on this' (Q1FY26).
- Binary dependence on certification with no revenue visibility until approval.
- Kavach division losses widened to ₹10.09 Cr from ₹4.24 Cr YoY before any revenue (Q1FY26).

## Overall Investment Signal
