Overall, execution on growth was solid, but the lack of detailed operational guidance and commentary limits transparency signals on medium-term strategy.

## Guidance Evolution Across Quarters
*Across the three quarters, management largely avoided giving quantified forward guidance. Communication focused on reporting outcomes rather than setting and revising explicit targets, limiting the ability to track guidance consistency.*

## Margin Commentary
Margin discussion across quarters was implicit through reported numbers rather than explicit guidance. Q1FY22 showed strong EBITDA margin expansion to 13.3%, Q3FY22 saw significant compression to 7.54% YoY, and Q4FY22 margins recovered to 10.08%. Management did not clearly articulate structural margin levers or long-term margin targets, which reduces visibility on margin sustainability.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Revenue growth narrative remained consistent and recovery-led. Q1FY22 highlighted a 94% YoY jump in total income, Q3FY22 showed continued growth albeit with margin pressure, and FY22 ended with revenue up 41% YoY. Growth was presented as organic, driven by higher production and sales volumes across duplex and writing & printing paper, with no mention of inorganic expansion.
