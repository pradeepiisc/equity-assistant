# Transcript Analysis — Updater Services Ltd (UDS)

## Executive Summary
Across the last four quarters, Updater Services Ltd has transitioned from a strong FY25 exit with margin expansion to a far more mixed FY26 marked by BSS stress, governance lapses at Avon, and repeated recalibration of growth guidance. IFM remains the stabilising pillar with steady 10–14% growth and structural tailwinds, while BSS has faced client insourcing, IT-sector slowdown, AI disruption, and an unexpected receivables write-off. Management has been candid about challenges, but guidance on growth and margins has drifted materially over the year. Overall, the narrative has shifted from confident growth to damage control and stabilization, with credibility hinging on recovery in BSS and tighter controls.

## Guidance Evolution Across Quarters
*Management set aggressive aspirations early, especially in BSS, and subsequently walked them back as realities emerged. IFM guidance shows better calibration, while BSS guidance has been overly optimistic and then sharply revised.*

### BSS growth trajectory — Reversed
- **Q4FY25:** BSS expected to grow >15% organically in FY26
- **Q1FY26:** BSS aimed to grow at ~15% CAGR long term
- **Q2FY26:** BSS facing near-term challenges, soft landing expected
- **Q3FY26:** BSS growth for FY26 revised down to ~3–4%

*Assessment: Management materially overestimated BSS resilience and had to sharply cut expectations.*

### Avon logistics business — Reversed
- **Q4FY25:** Avon crossed INR100 Cr revenue with margin improvement
- **Q2FY26:** INR280m receivables under review, INR30m provision taken
- **Q3FY26:** Total provisions of ~INR230m taken, logistics operations halted

*Assessment: What was positioned as a growth engine turned into a full shutdown, raising control concerns.*

### IFM growth outlook — Gradually Revised
- **Q4FY25:** IFM to grow at ~3x nominal GDP
- **Q1FY26:** Targeting 13–15% IFM growth
- **Q2FY26:** Targeting 10–12% IFM growth
- **Q3FY26:** IFM delivered 14% growth, guidance maintained at ~10–12%

*Assessment: Guidance moderated but largely delivered, showing better realism in IFM.*

## Margin Commentary
Margins were a key highlight exiting FY25, but FY26 saw pressure, particularly in BSS. Management consistently cited mix shift, IT hiring slowdown, AI-led disruption, and client insourcing as margin drags in Denave, Athena, and EBGC. IFM margins were impacted by ramp-up costs and strategic lower-margin contracts but are expected to normalize. Credibility is mixed: IFM margin commentary has tracked outcomes reasonably, while BSS margin recovery has been repeatedly deferred.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Revenue growth has increasingly leaned on IFM, driven by industrial outsourcing, GCC expansion, and formalization tailwinds. BSS growth narrative shifted from organic + tech-led acceleration to stabilization amid macro and tech disruption. Inorganic growth via acquisitions remains a stated lever, but no completed deals in FY26 so far. The growth story has become less balanced and more defensive over time.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- IT hiring slowdown impacting EBGC (Q4FY25, Q1FY26, Q2FY26, Q3FY26)
- AI and automation disrupting sales enablement and telesales models (Q4FY25 onwards)
- Client insourcing in Athena, especially BFSI (Q4FY25, Q1FY26)
- Receivables and due diligence lapses in Avon logistics (Q2FY26, Q3FY26)
- Working capital stretch due to upfront staffing for IFM contracts (Q1FY26, Q2FY26)

## Positive Signals
- IFM returned to 14% YoY growth in Q3FY26 with record quarterly revenue
- Net cash balance maintained despite volatility (Q1FY26, Q3FY26)
- Audit & Assurance business showing consistent growth and marquee client wins (Q4FY25, Q1FY26, Q3FY26)
- Formalization via new labour codes structurally favoring organized IFM players (Q3FY26)

## Red Flags
- Sharp escalation of Avon provisions from INR30m to ~INR230m within two quarters
- Repeated overestimation of BSS growth and margin recovery
- Frequent reliance on ‘macro’ and ‘technology uncertainty’ without clear timelines
- Lack of granular segment disclosure until pressed by analysts

## Overall Investment Signal
**cautious: IFM offers steady, defensible growth, but repeated BSS missteps, governance issues at Avon, and guidance volatility warrant a conservative stance until execution stabilizes.**
