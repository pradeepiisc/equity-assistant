## Margin Commentary
Management highlighted a sharp margin expansion in FY26, with operating margin rising to ~21% and PAT margin exceeding 10%, attributing gains to higher export realizations, favorable product mix, and backward integration via EC sheds. They emphasized that the ₹400 Cr poultry capex is largely bottom-line accretive, citing savings of ~20–50 paise per egg versus market procurement. Margin commentary was detailed and credible, though implicitly cyclical given dependence on global egg supply disruptions.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
FY26 revenue growth was overwhelmingly organic, driven by export volumes and realizations amid global supply disruptions (bird flu in US/Europe). Management acknowledged limited near-term volume growth due to full capacity, shifting the narrative toward mix improvement, shell egg exports (~₹100 Cr), domestic branded eggs (~₹40 Cr), and long-term capacity additions rather than immediate top-line acceleration.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- Bird flu outbreaks causing extreme volatility in global egg supply and prices (Q4FY26).
- High dependence on international markets and absence of stable global pricing benchmarks (Q4FY26).
- Manpower intensity and inefficiency in conventional poultry systems without automation (Q4FY26).
- FX volatility leading to partial reduction in hedging coverage to 30% currently (Q4FY26).
