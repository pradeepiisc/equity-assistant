# Transcript Analysis — MPS Ltd (MPSLTD)

## Executive Summary
Across Q1–Q4 FY26, MPS delivered margin-led earnings growth despite uneven revenue, with FY26 becoming its most profitable year ever (30.7% EBITDA margin). Management executed a deliberate reset—pruning AJE revenues, restructuring Corporate Learning, and doubling down on AI-first workflows—culminating in strong Q4 momentum and confident FY27 EBITDA guidance (>₹300 cr). The Unbound Medicine acquisition marks a strategic expansion into recurring, regulated healthcare knowledge platforms. Overall, management credibility is supported by consistent margin delivery, transparent acknowledgement of missteps (AJE, Corporate Learning), and increasingly specific forward commitments.

## Management Tone — IMPROVING
- Q1FY26: Management acknowledged a 'soft start' and explicitly called out AJE as an overhang rather than masking weakness.
- Q2FY26: Rahul Arora stated Corporate Learning had been a 'soft year' and framed FY26 as a restructuring/reset year.
- Q4FY26: Management gave explicit EBITDA guidance for FY27 (>₹300 cr) and stated it was 'built bottom-up' and already in execution.
- Feb2026 (Unbound call): Clear, quantified margin ramp guidance for Unbound (14% to MPS average by FY27 exit), showing confidence and specificity.
- Q4FY26: Capital allocation explanation (no final dividend due to deployment cycle) was candid and principle-based.

## Guidance vs Delivery
### Q1FY26 — DELIVERED
**Promised:** Margins to expand through FY26 despite revenue softness; AJE margins to improve materially.
**Delivered:** FY26 EBITDA margin expanded to 30.7%; AJE margins improved from ~23% to ~30% by Q2/Q4 commentary.

### Q2FY26 — DELIVERED
**Promised:** H2 stronger than H1; Corporate Learning margins to improve post restructuring.
**Delivered:** Q4FY26 saw Corporate Learning EBITDA up 55% sequentially with first positive revenue print of the year.

### Q4FY26 — PENDING
**Promised:** FY27 EBITDA to comfortably cross ₹300 cr at ~30–35% margin.
**Delivered:** FY27 results pending; operating plans stated to already be in flight.

## Guidance Evolution Across Quarters
*Management generally sets conservative-to-realistic targets, openly resets narratives when needed (AJE, Corporate Learning), and then delivers on revised expectations rather than silently deferring.*

### AJE margin and reset — Consistent
- **Q1FY26:** Revenue intentionally cut; focus on profitability over growth.
- **Q2FY26:** AJE margins expanded from ~23% to ~30%.
- **Q4FY26:** Pruning complete; base now margin-accretive and positioned for growth in FY27.

*Assessment: Management executed a painful but successful reset, with margins improving as promised.*

### Corporate Learning turnaround — Gradually Revised
- **Q1FY26:** Turnaround planned; margins improving ahead of revenue.
- **Q2FY26:** FY26 acknowledged as a soft year; consolidation underway.
- **Q4FY26:** Q4 inflection achieved; FY27 positioned for recovery.

*Assessment: Initial optimism gave way to realism, but execution improved by Q4.*

### Unbound Medicine integration and margins — Consistent
- **Feb2026:** 14% EBITDA at entry; ramp to MPS average by FY27 exit.
- **Q4FY26:** 50-day contribution delivered ~19% margin; ramp on track.

*Assessment: Early delivery supports credibility of margin ramp claims.*

## Margin Commentary
Margins were the central management focus across quarters. Management consistently cited AI-driven automation, workflow redesign, offshoring, and portfolio pruning as margin levers. Credibility is high: EBITDA margins expanded from ~28–31% in early FY26 to 32.9% in Q4 and 30.7% for FY26, validating repeated claims that AI and operating leverage would structurally lift margins.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Revenue growth in FY26 was uneven and largely margin-sacrificed in AJE and Corporate Learning, while Education saw strong organic growth (28–60%). The narrative shifted from acquisition-led scale to disciplined, AI-enabled organic expansion plus selective, higher-quality M&A (Unbound). This shift has been consistent since mid-FY26.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- Corporate Learning softness and restructuring risk (Q1FY26, Q2FY26).
- AJE revenue volatility during margin reset (Q1FY26, Q2FY26).
- Execution risk in integrating acquisitions and scaling AI responsibly in regulated workflows (Q4FY26, Feb2026).

## Positive Signals
- FY26 highest-ever EBITDA (₹236 cr) and EPS (₹102) – Q4FY26.
- Education segment delivered 36%+ organic growth with ~39% EBITDA margin – Q4FY26.
- Unbound Medicine added recurring revenue and new healthcare vertical – Feb2026.
- DSO improvement from ~60 days to ~37–45 days – Q1–Q2FY26.
- Clear FY27 EBITDA guidance (>₹300 cr) – Q4FY26.

## Red Flags
- Repeated deferral of revenue growth recovery at AJE until FY27 (Q1–Q4FY26).
- Corporate Learning turnaround took longer than initially suggested (Q1 vs Q2FY26).
- Limited revenue disclosure on Unbound initially, citing account closure timing (Feb2026).

## Overall Investment Signal
**positive: Margin execution, AI monetisation, and disciplined capital allocation underpin confidence, though revenue acceleration still needs to prove consistency post-FY26 reset.**
