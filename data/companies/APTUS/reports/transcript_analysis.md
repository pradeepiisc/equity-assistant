# Transcript Analysis — Aptus Value Housing Finance Ltd (APTUS)

## Executive Summary
Across FY25 Q4 to FY26 Q3, Aptus has delivered steady AUM growth (~21–25% YoY), best-in-class profitability (ROA ~7.9%, ROE ~20%+), and stable asset quality despite conscious growth moderation. Management has proactively recalibrated strategy—exiting sub-₹7 lakh tickets, accelerating branch expansion, and tightening write-off policies—to protect portfolio quality even at the cost of near-term growth. Tone remains confident and data-driven, though growth guidance has been revised down incrementally, reflecting realism rather than denial. Key themes across calls are disciplined risk management, productivity-led scaling, and sustained margin leadership.

## Margin Commentary
Margins have been a consistent strength. Management highlighted declining cost of funds (from ~8.6% to ~8.3%) aided by repo cuts and rating upgrades, while largely retaining asset yields. Rate cuts were selectively passed on only to incremental home loans, limiting NIM impact to <10 bps. Across quarters, margin commentary has been granular and credible, supported by actual spread expansion to ~8.9% by Q3FY26.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Revenue growth has been largely organic, driven by branch expansion, higher average ticket size, and productivity improvements. Management consistently cited Tier 3/4 housing demand, self-employed customer focus, and digital sourcing as growth drivers. The narrative shifted in FY26 toward quality over volume, with deliberate exit from sub-₹7 lakh loans even as it dampened short-term disbursements.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- Field-level staff attrition impacting productivity (Q1FY26)
- Rising competition in affordable housing, especially from banks and SFBs (Q1FY26, Q3FY26)
- Seasonal volatility affecting collections (Q1FY26, Q3FY26)
- Temporary stress in MSME portfolio due to end-use diversion (Q3FY26)

## Positive Signals
- CARE rating upgrade to AA improving funding access (Q1FY26)
- ROE consistently above 20%, among the highest in the sector (all quarters)
- Successful expansion into Maharashtra and Odisha with improving traction (Q4FY25–Q3FY26)
- Cost of funds declined by ~40 bps over FY26 (Q3FY26)

## Red Flags
- Repeated downward revision of growth guidance (from ~30% to ~22–24%)
- Visibility on monthly disbursement trends often deferred with limited quantification
- Dependence on favorable rate cycle for margin expansion

## Overall Investment Signal
**positive: Aptus combines superior profitability, conservative risk management, and transparent governance. While growth has moderated, this reflects strategic discipline rather than business weakness.**
