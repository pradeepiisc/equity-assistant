# Transcript Analysis — ACLD (ACLD)

## Executive Summary
Across H2 FY25 and H1 FY26, ACLD (Aelea Commodities) shows a sharp inflection from a disrupted expansion year to a strong scale-up phase. FY25 was marred by regulatory delays and sub-scale operations that compressed margins and led to guidance miss, but H1 FY26 demonstrates clean execution with >100% YoY revenue growth and return to positive operating leverage. Management’s core themes—scale, full integration, and margin focus over topline—remain consistent, with clearer articulation and better delivery in FY26. Overall, management credibility improves as operational bottlenecks ease, though the business remains exposed to commodity volatility and execution risk in upcoming Phase 2/3 projects.

## Guidance vs Delivery
### H2FY25 — MISSED
**Promised:** Completion of capacity expansion by Dec 2024 and normalized margins of ~12–13%
**Delivered:** Expansion completed only by May 2025; margins compressed with negative EBITDA months

### H1FY26 — DELIVERED
**Promised:** Full utilization post-ramp-up and return to normalized margins
**Delivered:** 3.5 months at full capacity in H1; EBITDA margin 8.8% with expectation of normalization in H2

## Guidance Evolution Across Quarters
*Management sets aspirational timelines but has shown willingness to acknowledge delays and recalibrate without abandoning targets. Post-FY25, guidance realism has improved.*

### 140 TPD capacity ramp-up — Consistent
- **H2FY25:** Entire plant commissioned end of May; full utilization expected by July first week.
- **H1FY26:** Operated at full capacity for ~3.5 months; currently at 140 TPD consistently.

*Assessment: Initial delay acknowledged, but once commissioned, ramp-up happened as guided.*

### EBITDA margin normalization — Gradually Revised
- **H2FY25:** Target to return to 12–13% margins once capacity is fully utilized.
- **H1FY26:** H1 margins impacted by initial months; full-year margins of ~11–12% achievable.

*Assessment: Margin targets broadly maintained, but timelines softened due to execution delays.*

### CNSL / biofuel Phase 2 — Gradually Revised
- **H2FY25:** Phase 2 to start post-monsoon and complete before FY26 end.
- **H1FY26:** Phase 2 in advanced preparatory stage; completion by end FY26 or early FY27.

*Assessment: Project remains on roadmap but timelines have been pushed modestly.*

## Margin Commentary
Margins were the central concern in H2 FY25, with management attributing pressure to sub-scale operations, forced reliance on intermediaries, and commodity price correction. By H1 FY26, commentary shifts to operating leverage, with EBITDA margins recovering to ~8.8% despite only partial-period utilization. Management consistently reiterates a normalized 11–13% EBITDA potential at steady-state, which appears credible now that full capacity is operational.

## Revenue Growth Narrative
Revenue growth has pivoted from trading-led volatility to processing-led scale. FY25 revenues of ₹182 Cr were constrained by delays, while H1 FY26 saw >100% YoY growth driven entirely by cashew processing with zero trading revenue. Management consistently emphasizes volume-led growth with commodity prices as a swing factor, positioning future growth as largely organic through capacity utilization rather than diversification.

## Key Risks Flagged by Management
- Regulatory and approval delays impacting project timelines (H2FY25)
- Commodity price volatility affecting reported margins and topline (H2FY25, H1FY26)
- Working capital intensity due to large base inventory requirements (H2FY25, H1FY26)
- Dependence on imported raw cashew nuts from Africa (H2FY25)

## Positive Signals
- Successful commissioning and ramp-up of 140 TPD automated plant (H1FY26)
- Revenue growth of 110% YoY and PAT growth of 135% YoY in H1FY26
- Exit from low-margin trading business, now 100% processing-led (H1FY26)
- Reduction in borrowings by ~₹12 Cr improving balance sheet strength (H1FY26)
- CRISIL BBB stable rating reaffirmed (H2FY25)

## Red Flags
- Repeated refusal to give concrete revenue/profit guidance citing commodity nature
- Initial over-optimism around expansion timelines leading to FY25 miss
- Heavy reliance on management narrative rather than disclosed KPIs like EBITDA/ton

## Overall Investment Signal
**positive: Post a difficult FY25 marked by execution issues, ACLD has demonstrated strong operational recovery and scale benefits in H1 FY26. Improved delivery against guidance and margin normalization support a constructive view, albeit with commodity and execution risks.**
