## TARIL vs QPOWER — Are They the Same?

**No — they make fundamentally different products:**

| | QPOWER | TARIL |
|---|---|---|
| **Product** | HVDC converters, FACTS controllers, GIS switchgear | Distribution & power transformers, rectifiers |
| **Function** | Grid management / power flow control | Voltage conversion (step-up / step-down) |
| **Primary customer** | Grid operators, transmission utilities, renewable EPC | Utilities, industrials, EPC contractors, data centres, railways |
| **Renewable link** | Strongly tied — HVDC for offshore wind, FACTS for intermittent grid stability | Used across ALL applications — renewables, data centres, EV charging, industry |
| **India tailwind** | Energy transition / green grid modernisation | Entire power capex cycle (not just renewables) |

QPOWER is a specialised play on the renewable energy transition. TARIL is a "picks and shovels" play on the entire Indian electricity economy — every factory, data centre, EV charger and household needs transformers. They are complementary, not duplicative. Holding QPOWER does NOT mean the transformer manufacturing sector is covered.
